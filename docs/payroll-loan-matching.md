# Intelligent Loan Adapter

Open Loans > Bulk Repayments > Intelligent Loan Adapter (admin/treasurer with repayment permission).

1. Choose a month and Export Loan Book. This lists active outstanding loans with member/staff numbers, names, recorded loan amounts, balances, expected instalments and the schedule basis. Migrated opening balances are not necessarily original disbursement amounts. Where schedules are missing or migrated, the latest historical monthly payment total is suggested, capped at the outstanding balance. The most recent three recorded months from the preceding 12 months are shown as evidence. One-month and irregular history are explicitly flagged for review. No usable history means an unconfirmed amount.
2. Upload HR's original single-sheet XLSX or UTF-8 CSV. The combined schedule recognises `Employee Number`, `Full name`, `COOPERATIVE LOAN`, `COOPERATIVE SAVINGS` and `COOPERATIVE SUBSCRIPTION`. It ignores paygrade and sequence columns. Trailing per-category and grand totals are removed only after they reconcile exactly (to cents) to the employee rows; a discrepancy blocks processing. Loan-only files using `amount` plus `member_number`, `staff_number` or `member_name` remain supported. IDs with leading zeros should be Excel text.
3. Choose the payroll month, actual payment date and debit account once. Select 1400 only when it is the cooperative's configured payroll control account.
4. Review identity, savings and loan allocations together. Unique names can match regardless of name order. HR employee numbers are checked against staff and migrated member numbers; collisions or conflicting names require manual confirmation. A missing/different name can be resolved in the Member Match selector, then Apply Member Matches. This refreshes allocation suggestions, so finish identity matching before editing splits. IDs never silently fall back to names. Multiple loans are prefilled only when all known expected instalments equal the deduction. Otherwise enter the split explicitly. Future instalments are shown as zero; explicit early repayment remains possible.
5. Confirm each included member and deduction or explicitly exclude it. Loans and savings can be excluded independently; a loan problem need not hold up a valid savings deduction. Loan allocations must equal the HR loan deduction exactly. The savings amount is taken from the HR savings column, never from the loan/subscription columns.
6. Download Loan & Savings Schedules. Combined payroll produces a ZIP containing `loan_repayments_YYYY-MM.csv`, `salary_savings_YYYY-MM.csv`, a per-employee reconciliation report and import instructions. Loan-only files still produce the existing loan CSV. Extract the ZIP; import the loan CSV in Bulk Repayments and the savings CSV in Salary Upload. Do not resave the CSVs in Excel. Subscriptions are retained as separate, unposted fees in the reconciliation report, not silently treated as savings. Any cooperative share-capital split is applied by the normal savings importer.

Preparation never posts money. Preview tokens expire after one hour, are signed and tied to the preparing user and host. Combined schedules also require savings-management permission. Current balances/status and savings identifiers are checked again at export. Existing repayments or savings in the payroll month block that deduction category, including legacy receipts; investigate those via the existing workflows. Stable per-month/member/loan and per-month/member/savings receipts protect retries of adapter-generated files. The posting importers still perform their own validation and receipt checks.

Export audit history records the preparing user, approved allocation amounts, account, date, receipts and excluded source rows. Corrections to prior postings are separate controlled actions. A reviewed file is not proof of a successful posting: check Upload History after import.

Limits: 5 MB files, 2,000 input rows, one worksheet; no fuzzy name auto-allocation. The adapter does not correct historic rates or migrated schedules and does not include arrears in the scheduled monthly instalment automatically.

Historical suggestions exclude reversed, non-positive and explicitly labelled pre-liquidation, settlement or catch-up payments. Current and future payroll months are excluded. Unlabelled catch-up or combined payments can still distort history, so historical totals are suggestions, not contractual instalments; the treasurer must confirm them. This is transparent rule-based matching, not an external AI service: member data is not sent to a third party.
