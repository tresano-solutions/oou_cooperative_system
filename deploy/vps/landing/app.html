Installing it

Four steps, about two minutes. You will need the short code your cooperative uses — ask your secretary if you do not know it.

Download

Tap the button above on your Android phone. The file is about 60 MB, so use Wi-Fi if you can.

Allow the install

Android will ask whether to allow installing from your browser. Tap Settings, turn it on, then go back.

Enter your code

On first launch, enter the cooperative code provided by your secretary or administrator.

Sign in

Use the same email and password as the member portal. Forgotten it? Tap Forgot password.

On iPhone

On iPhone, use the CoopMS web portal in Safari and add it to your Home Screen for convenient access to your cooperative. No App Store download is needed for this option.

Open your portal

In Safari, open CoopMS Sign in and enter the code supplied by your cooperative administrator.

Tap Share

The square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen.

Add to Home Screen

Scroll down the list and tap it, then tap Add.

Open it like an app

Your cooperative's icon is now on your Home Screen. Tap it and sign in as usual.

A full iPhone app is coming to the App Store. Anything you set up now carries over — nothing is lost.

Why Android warns you

Because this app is downloaded from our website rather than the Play Store, Android shows a warning about installing from an unknown source. That warning is about where the file came from, not about the app.

Only ever download it from cooperativems.com. If someone sends you a CoopMS file by WhatsApp or email, do not install it — come here instead.

Once we are on Google Play the warning disappears, and you will be able to move across without losing anything.

Questions

Will it update itself?

Yes, mostly. Improvements and fixes arrive on their own the next time you open the app — you do not have to download anything again. Bigger changes occasionally need a fresh download, and we will tell you inside the app when that happens.

Is my money information safe?

Your connection is encrypted, your bank details and identity numbers are encrypted in storage, and card numbers are never held by us at all. Read the privacy policy for the full picture.

Do I need a separate app for my cooperative?

No. One app serves every society using CoopMS. Your code decides which one you see, and you can switch societies from the sign-in screen.

What about iPhone?

Apple does not allow installing apps from a website, so there is no download for iPhone yet. Add the portal to your Home Screen instead — 30-second instructions — and it behaves like an app. A full App Store version is coming.

I do not know my cooperative's code

Ask your secretary or treasurer. It is usually a short version of the society's name, and it is the same code used in your portal web address.
