1. Who is responsible for your information

CoopMS is software operated by Adekail Professional Services (“we”, “us”), registered in Nigeria at [REGISTERED ADDRESS].

Two different organisations have a role in your information, and the difference matters:

  • Your cooperative society is the data controller. It decides what information to collect from its members and what to do with it. It holds your membership records because you are a member of it.
  • We are the data processor. We provide the software your society uses, and we handle your information only on its instructions. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Each society runs on its own separate database. Your information is never mixed with, or visible to, another cooperative.

If you want your record corrected or removed, contact your cooperative first — they control it. We will help them act on your request.

2. What we collect

We collect only what a cooperative needs to run your membership and your money.

What we never collect

  • Your card number. Card payments are handled entirely by a licensed payment provider. We never see or store card numbers, expiry dates or CVVs.
  • Your location. Neither the app nor the website tracks where you are.
  • Your contacts, photos, messages or other apps. The app does not read them.
  • Advertising or tracking data. There is no advertising in the app, and no third-party analytics inside it.

4. The mobile app

The CoopMS app is a window onto your own membership record. It shows you the same information your cooperative already holds, and lets you update your details, apply for a loan, and see what you have paid in.

What the app stores on your phone

  • A sign-in token, held in your device’s encrypted secure storage and valid for 24 hours.
  • The code of the cooperative you chose, so you do not have to type it again.

Nothing else is kept on the device. Signing out removes both.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a token that lets us send alerts to your phone. It identifies the device, not you personally, and cannot be used to read anything on it. Turning notifications off in your phone settings stops them.

Your personal account number

If your cooperative issues account numbers, the app shows yours. It is a real bank account in your name at a partner bank, used only to receive money you send to your cooperative. You choose in the app whether those transfers pay for savings, a loan, or a Target Advance contribution.

6. Where your information is stored

Our servers are located in Germany, which means your information is transferred outside Nigeria. We do this because it gives the service better reliability and security than we could otherwise provide.

The Nigeria Data Protection Act allows such transfers where the receiving country offers an adequate level of protection or where appropriate contractual safeguards are in place. Our hosting provider is bound by data-protection terms that meet this requirement.

7. How long we keep it

  • While you are a member — for as long as your membership lasts.
  • After you leave — your financial records are kept for six years, because cooperative and tax rules require the books to be auditable for that period.
  • Sign-in and activity logs — 12 months.
  • Device notification tokens — until you sign out or uninstall the app.

After those periods, records are deleted or anonymised.

8. How we protect it

  • Encrypted in transit. Every connection to the app and the website uses HTTPS.
  • Sensitive fields encrypted at rest. Bank account details, BVN and NIN are encrypted in the database, so they are unreadable even to someone with direct access to it.
  • Passwords are never stored. Only a one-way cryptographic hash is kept, which cannot be turned back into your password.
  • Two-factor authentication is available, and your cooperative can require it for officers.
  • Separate databases. Each cooperative's data is physically separated from every other's.
  • Access is logged. Every change to a member record records who made it and when.
  • Regular backups, so your society's records survive a hardware failure.

No system is completely secure. If a breach occurs that is likely to put you at risk, we will notify your cooperative and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission as the law requires, and your cooperative will notify you.

9. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you have the right to:

  • Know what information is held about you and get a copy of it.
  • Correct anything that is wrong or out of date.
  • Ask for deletion, where there is no legal or contractual reason to keep it.
  • Object to certain uses of your information, and withdraw consent you previously gave.
  • Receive your data in a portable, machine-readable form.
  • Complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission if you believe your rights have been breached.

Most of these you can exercise yourself: you can view and correct your own details in the app or the member portal at any time. For anything else, contact your cooperative, or us at the address below.

We will respond within 30 days. There is no charge for a reasonable request.

10. Deleting your account and data

To ask for your data to be deleted:

  • Email privacy@cooperativems.com from the address on your membership record, or
  • Ask your cooperative's secretary to make the request on your behalf.

We will confirm within 30 days. Please note that your cooperative must keep your financial records for the statutory period in section 7 even after you leave — those cannot be deleted on request, because the law requires the books to balance. Everything not covered by that obligation will be removed.

Uninstalling the app removes it from your phone but does not delete your membership record, which is held by your cooperative.

11. Children

CoopMS is for adult members of cooperative societies. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If a child's information reaches us in error, contact us and we will remove it.

12. Our website

This website uses Google Tag Manager to understand how visitors find and use our marketing pages. That is separate from the app and the member portal, neither of which contains any third-party analytics.

If you fill in our demo request form, we use the details you give to contact you about CoopMS. We do not add you to marketing lists without asking.
