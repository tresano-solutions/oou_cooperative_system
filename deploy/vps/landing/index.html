FAQ

Questions, answered

Is this real accounting, or just a savings tracker?

It's real double-entry accounting. Every transaction posts a balanced journal entry, and you get a trial balance, income statement, balance sheet and cash flow that always tie out — the same statements your auditor expects.

Do we have to leave our spreadsheets and current software?

Yes — that's the point. CoopMS replaces the spreadsheets and the separate accounting package with one system. We migrate your members, savings, loans and opening balances and reconcile them to your current trial balance.

Can members see their own balances?

Every member gets a secure portal to view savings, share capital and loan balances, download statements, apply for loans and respond to guarantor requests — which sharply reduces routine queries to your office.

How is our data protected?

Each society runs on its own secure site. Staff access is protected by two-factor authentication and role-based permissions, every action is logged, and finalised accounting periods can be locked.

How long does setup take?

Most societies go live within days. We handle migration and configuration; you review the reconciled figures and onboard your officers and members.

How much does it cost?

Pricing scales with your membership and plan. Tell us about your society and we'll send an exact quote with a demo.
